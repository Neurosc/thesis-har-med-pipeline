# Thesis Figures

This directory holds curated figures formatted for the thesis. Originals
live in `01_preprocessing/01_QC/figures/` and are not moved here — these are copies.

## supplementary/
Motion QC and preprocessing figures, all to appear in the supplementary
section of the thesis:

- `fig_S_motion_subject_level_fd.png`: per-subject mean FD across both sessions
- `fig_S_respiratory_check_group.png`: group-averaged motion parameter PSDs (low- vs high-motion subjects), demonstrating absence of respiratory peaks
- `fig_S_resp_example_low-motion.png`: example individual PSD (sub-39 ses-01, lowest mean FD)
- `fig_S_resp_example_high-motion.png`: example individual PSD (sub-12 ses-01, highest mean FD)
- `fig_S_excluded_sub-XX_ses-YY.png`: motion QC figure for each excluded run, showing FD, DVARS, and censored frames
